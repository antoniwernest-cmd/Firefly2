Find the chunks

Read syllables, then explore meaningful word parts.

Say each part, blend the whole word, then explain its meaning.

Do these parts make a familiar word?

Syllables are spoken beats: rab / bit.
Meaningful parts tell us more: wash + out → washout; over + night → overnight.
Overnight has three syllables. Word parts and syllables are different.
A little practice, often. Read a few words accurately, reread them smoothly, then read a sentence. “Read smoothly” is a self-check, not an automatic reading assessment. Progress stays in this browser.
